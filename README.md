# Blood on the Clocktower — Storyteller Grimoire

## What Is Blood on the Clocktower?

**Blood on the Clocktower** is a social deduction game of bluffing, mystery, and intrigue set in the sleepy mountain village of Ravenswood Bluff. Each game pits the **Good** team (Townsfolk and Outsiders) against the **Evil** team (Minions and the Demon) across alternating **Night** and **Day** phases.

Every player receives a unique secret character with a special ability—whether gathering hidden clues, protecting allies, spreading misinformation, or sowing chaos. Unlike many social deduction games, players are never eliminated from the conversation when they die: ghosts keep talking, plotting, and hold one final vote to sway a crucial execution. At the center of it all is the **Storyteller**, who secretly tracks the state of the town in the Grimoire and guides the village through every twist until either the Demon is executed or Evil overtakes the town.

## Implemented Scripts

The companion currently supports all three official base editions for **5–15 players** (with optional Teensyville rules for 5–6 players):

- **Trouble Brewing** — The classic introductory edition built around detective work, subtle misinformation, poisoning, and drunkenness. Ideal for new and mixed groups learning the rhythm of the game.
- **Bad Moon Rising** — A tense, lethal edition centered on night deaths, protection, resurrection, and execution survival, where figuring out *why* people did or did not die is the key to solving the puzzle.
- **Sects & Violets** — A fast-paced, mind-bending edition featuring madness, tangled information, shifting characters, and Demons that warp the rules of truth and alignment.

## How the App Works

This web app serves as a digital Grimoire and Storyteller companion designed for in-person games:

1. **Table Setup & Themes** — Choose a Storyteller nickname, select one of the three scripts, set the player count to see the official role distribution, and switch between three dark themes (**Gothic**, **Infernal**, and **Sanguine**).
2. **Character Selection** — Pick which characters will be in play (or randomize a balanced bag), with automatic distribution adjustments and setup prompts handled for script-specific roles like the Baron, Drunk, Godfather, Lunatic, Fang Gu, and Vigormortis.
3. **Pass-and-Play Role Draw** — Enter each player's name one at a time and hand them the device to draw a secret random character from the bag. Drawn roles are removed immediately without revealing previous draws, and hidden roles such as the Drunk and Lunatic are shown only as the character they believe themselves to be.
4. **Running the Game** — Manage the town from an interactive circular Grimoire where seats can be dragged and swapped to match the real-world circle. Track alive/dead states, removable ghost votes, poison, drunkenness, madness, and other script-specific statuses; choose and reveal Demon bluffs or character cards on a spoiler-safe full-screen overlay; and step through each edition's guided Night Order and Day trackers from the first night until the final reveal.
5. **Script Reference** — Browse every character's ability, Storyteller explanation, and First Night / Other Nights order whenever a quick rules check is needed.
