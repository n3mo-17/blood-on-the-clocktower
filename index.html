Ravenswood Bluff · Social Deduction

Blood on the Clocktower

In the quiet mountain village of Ravenswood Bluff, a Demon walks among the townsfolk—striking by night and hiding in plain sight by day.

Every player receives a unique secret character with a special ability—and even in death, ghosts retain a final vote and remain part of the intrigue. The Storyteller (GM) guides the town through night and day, tracks hidden truths in the Grimoire, and weaves a mystery no one will forget.

How the Night Unfolds

A quick refresher before gathering the village circle

1. Fall of Night

Everyone closes their eyes. The Storyteller wakes roles in night order to gather information, weave poison, or claim a victim for the Demon.

2. Dawn & Whispers

The town wakes to learn who died overnight. Players whisper in private, share claims publicly, and piece together who can be trusted.

3. Nominations & Execution

The town nominates suspects and casts votes around the clocktower. Even dead players hold one ghost vote to tip the final day.
